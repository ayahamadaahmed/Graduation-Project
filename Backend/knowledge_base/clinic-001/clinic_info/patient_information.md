# Dr. Maher Mahmoud Dermatology Clinic - Patient Information and Policies

Source type: MedMind demo provider configuration  
Provider ID: clinic-001  
Last updated: 2026-09-30

## Appointment Policy

Appointments can be booked through MedMind according to the current availability in the clinic scheduling system.

Patients are encouraged to arrive approximately 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

Appointment availability may vary depending on the doctor, service, branch, date, and time.

Current appointment availability must always be retrieved from the live MedMind scheduling system.

## Appointment Confirmation

Before an appointment is confirmed, the patient should verify the important booking details, including:

- Patient name
- Mobile number
- Doctor
- Appointment date
- Appointment time

After a successful booking, the patient may receive the confirmed appointment details through the configured communication channel.

## Cancellation Policy

Patients may request cancellation of an existing appointment.

Whenever possible, patients are encouraged to cancel at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment so that the time slot can become available to another patient.

Cancellation must be performed against the patient's current appointment record in the MedMind appointment system.

## Rescheduling Policy

Patients may request to reschedule an existing appointment.

The new appointment is subject to current availability.

The requested doctor, branch, date, or time may not be available when the appointment is rescheduled.

The new appointment details should be confirmed with the patient before the change is finalized.

## Late Arrival Policy

Patients are encouraged to arrive on time for their appointments.

If a patient expects to be late, they should contact the clinic when possible.

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may experience a delay or may need to reschedule depending on the clinic schedule and doctor availability.

## No-Show Policy

Patients who cannot attend an appointment are encouraged to cancel or reschedule in advance.

Repeated missed appointments without prior cancellation may require confirmation with clinic staff before another appointment is scheduled.

## Payment Methods

The clinic accepts cash payments.

Visa and Mastercard payments are also accepted at supported clinic locations.

Additional electronic payment methods may be available depending on the clinic branch.

Patients may confirm available payment methods with the clinic when necessary.

## Insurance

Insurance coverage depends on the patient's insurance provider, insurance plan, requested service, and clinic branch.

Patients are encouraged to verify their insurance coverage before receiving a service.

Insurance coverage for medical dermatology services does not necessarily include cosmetic dermatology, laser treatments, or cosmetic procedures.

When insurance eligibility or coverage cannot be confirmed by MedMind, the patient should be referred to clinic staff or the relevant insurance provider.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the service, payment status, and whether the service or procedure has already been provided.

Payment and refund exceptions should be reviewed by authorized clinic staff.

When applicable, an approved refund may be processed using the appropriate payment method.

## Preparing for a Clinic Visit

Patients are encouraged to bring relevant medical information when applicable, including:

- Previous medical reports
- Relevant laboratory or diagnostic results
- Previous prescriptions
- Information about current medications
- Information about known allergies
- Relevant previous dermatology treatment information

Patients should follow any specific preparation instructions provided by the clinic or treating healthcare professional.

Patients should not stop or change prescribed medication unless instructed by an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Dermatology and Cosmetic Procedures

Some dermatology, cosmetic, and laser procedures may require an assessment before treatment.

The appropriate treatment, procedure, and number of sessions depend on the individual patient's assessment.

The availability of a service does not mean that it is medically appropriate for every patient.

Medical suitability and treatment decisions should be determined by the treating healthcare professional.

## Pediatric Dermatology

The clinic provides dermatology care for children.

A parent or legal guardian should accompany a child when appropriate and provide relevant medical information.

## Patient Information and Privacy

Personal and medical information should only be accessed or used for authorized healthcare and administrative purposes.

Sensitive patient information should not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.

Identity verification may be required before MedMind provides protected patient information, medical records, or test results.

## Emergency Situations

MedMind and the clinic appointment service are not substitutes for emergency medical care.

A patient experiencing a medical emergency should contact the appropriate emergency medical service or go to the nearest emergency department.

The AI assistant should not delay emergency care in order to continue a normal booking or information flow.

## Human Support

Patients may request assistance from clinic staff when the AI assistant cannot complete their request.

Requests requiring exceptions to clinic policy, administrative approval, payment review, insurance verification, or other human authorization may be escalated to clinic staff.
