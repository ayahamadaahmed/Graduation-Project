## About

Dr. Maher Mahmoud is a board-certified dermatologist and consultant in dermatology, cosmetic medicine, and laser therapy with more than 30 years of experience.

The clinic provides medical and cosmetic dermatology services for adults and children.

## Services

The clinic provides services including:

- Adult dermatology
- Pediatric dermatology
- Cosmetic dermatology and laser treatments
- Acne and acne-scar treatment
- Melasma and pigmentation treatment
- Hair-loss treatment
- Laser hair removal
- Chemical peels and facials
- Botox and fillers
- Skin cancer screening
- Eczema and psoriasis care
