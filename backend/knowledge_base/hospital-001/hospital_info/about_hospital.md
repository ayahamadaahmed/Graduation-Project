## About

Cairo Specialized Hospital is a private healthcare facility in Egypt and has been providing healthcare services for more than four decades.

The hospital is part of Cleopatra Hospitals Group.

## Centers of Excellence

The hospital hosts specialized centers of excellence in:

- Orthopedics
- Neuroscience

## Hospital Capacity

According to the hospital's official website:

- 188 beds
- Approximately 600 medical staff
- More than 200,000 cases served
- Around 8,000 surgeries performed
