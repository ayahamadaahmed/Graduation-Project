# Cairo Specialized Hospital - Patient Rights

Source type: Official Cleopatra Hospitals Group website
Source URL: https://cleopatrahospitals.com/en/patient-area/patients-rights/
Retrieved: 2026-08-19

## Respect and Privacy

Patients have the right to respectful and considerate care.

Patient privacy should be maintained during examinations,
assessments, procedures, and treatment.

## Information About Care

Patients have the right to receive understandable information
from their physician about their diagnosis, treatment,
prognosis, and significant changes in their condition.

Patients also have the right to know the name of the physician
responsible for their treatment or procedure.

## Informed Consent

Before a medical procedure or treatment, patients have the
right to receive information needed to make an informed
decision.

This includes information about the proposed procedure or
treatment, important risks, available alternatives, and the
expected outcome.

## Costs and Billing

Patients have the right to receive information about the cost
of healthcare services provided by the hospital.

Patients may also request an itemized bill explaining charges.

## Confidentiality

Medical records and information related to patient care should
be treated as confidential, except when disclosure is required
by law or authorized by the patient.

## Medical Reports and Test Results

Patients may request a medical report and copies of their
medical test results through the Medical Records Department,
subject to the hospital's authorization requirements.

## Second Opinion

Patients have the right to request assistance in obtaining a
consultation or second medical opinion from another physician.

## Refusing Treatment

Patients have the right to refuse treatment.

When refusing treatment may affect the ability to provide
appropriate care, the possible consequences should be
explained to the patient.

## Complaints and Concerns

The hospital states that patient concerns and complaints should
be handled fairly by hospital administration and staff.
