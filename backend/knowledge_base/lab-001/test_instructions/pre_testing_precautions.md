# Alfa Labs - Pre-Testing Precautions

Source type: Official laboratory website
Source URL: https://alfalaboratory.com/pre-testing-precautions/
Retrieved: 2026-08-19

## General Guidance

Some laboratory tests require specific preparation before
the sample is collected.

Patients should follow the instructions provided for the
specific requested test and follow their physician's
instructions when applicable.

Medication should not be stopped or changed unless the
physician or laboratory specifically instructs the patient
to do so.

## Fasting Blood Glucose

According to Alfa Labs, fasting blood glucose may require
approximately 6 to 12 hours of fasting.

Water is permitted during the fasting period.

## Lipid Profile

A lipid profile may require approximately 12 to 16 hours
of fasting.

Water is permitted during the fasting period.

## Blood Cholesterol

Blood cholesterol testing may require approximately
6 to 16 hours of fasting.

## Urine Pregnancy Test

The laboratory recommends using an early-morning urine
sample because the pregnancy hormone is generally more
concentrated in the first urine of the day.

## 24-Hour Urine Collection

For tests requiring a 24-hour urine collection, the
collection period starts after emptying the bladder and
recording the start time.

Urine is then collected through the same time on the
following day.

The laboratory's instructions state that the collected
urine should be kept refrigerated until it is delivered
to the laboratory.

## Sample-Specific Instructions

Some laboratory tests have additional preparation,
collection, storage, or timing requirements.

Patients should confirm the requirements for their
specific test with the laboratory before collecting
or submitting the sample.
